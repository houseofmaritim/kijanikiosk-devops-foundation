# Q. EXPLAIN REGION SELECTION AND MULTI-AZ RELIABILITY THINKING
For KijaniKioski, I would initially operate within a single region, since most of its customers are based in Kenya and neighboring African countries. Within that region, I would use 2–3 availability zones(multiple AZ) to ensure high availability, fault tolerance, data redundancy, backups, disaster recovery, and improved system reliability.

As the platform grows and begins to attract users from other continents, I would recommend expanding into additional regions such as North America or Europe based on where the majority of new users are located. This approach would help reduce latency, improve user experience, and ensure compliance with regional data regulations while maintaining scalability and performance while ensuring cost-effectiveness.
